Ducks in a Row (StormHacks 2025)

Ducks in a Row is a parody of Pico Park birthed in October 2025 during a 24-Hour hackathon. Using Unity as our game engine and taking advantage of the free tilesets from the store, we were able to create a semi-polished minigame that brought a crowd to our table during presentation time.

Levels:

Level 0: Players are first met with a large pit that requires a bit of skill to jump over. Then, they learn the lesson that this game is impossible if you don't work together. Players must climb on top of one another to allow one person to jump up push the box off the cliff so everyone can escape together. Level 1: After climbing down the ladder, players must work together to cross the pit using a mechanic where a bridge appears only when a player stands on the scarecrow. No ducks left behind.
Level 2: The last level is just a silly level. You just climb. BUT, when you get to the top, you'll find an easter egg. This is a reference to a world famous block game and the infamous portal to another world. This portal isn't as it seems, as the end is actually hidden behind the cliff and you'll only escape if you find it.
