My Story

Growing up, I always knew that I was a creative person. I'd like to believe it was due to the fact that, when I was younger, my older sister and I would frequently pull all of our plushies off our beds and play dolls. Together, we would build a world revolving around the characteristics of our toys, each story unique from the last, and it would last all day if we were knees deep into a story that required a resolution. I don't remember what age it was that we decided we were too old to play with dolls, but it was around the same time we grew apart and into our own individual selves.
We still had one common interest— drawing— but was it more hers than mine, as I found mine in video games instead. I occassionally sketched, but never had any artwork whose "finished state" came close to what my sister had achieved. Not that I ever let that discourage me or anything. Regardless, my passion for storywriting never died, as I would daydream about fantastical stories of knights and dragons or supernatural stories with vampires and werewolves. Those stories will never leave the Google Docs though.
Since my life has been influenced by a mix of art and video gaming, I always thought I would pursue a career in video game development. However, I find my interests waivering these days, as I have developed a curious interest in machine learning. Perhaps my future will land me in a place where machine learning and video games co-exist.
As of now, I'm in my fourth year studying Computer Science at Simon Fraser University.
If I'm not doing homework, I'm playing video games or pitching project/game ideas to my friends that never make it to the actual development phase.
